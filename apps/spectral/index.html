Spectral

A wallpaper app for Android. Don't pick a wallpaper, build one: stack overlays on a curated photo or a generated background and make a home screen that's entirely yours.

Get it on Google Play
  • Free, with ads
  • Spectral Pro: one-time purchase, no ads
  • Out now on Android

Inside the app.

Stack overlays
like layers in Photoshop.

Glass, liquid, stripes and patterns: pile them on any image or generated background. Every layer is non-destructive and fully tunable. Pull a slider, and commit when it feels right.

  • Layer any overlay on any image, or on nothing at all
  • Every parameter is live: drag a slider and see it update
  • Premium overlays such as liquid glass, droplets and stripes come with Pro

The rest of the toolkit

Wallpaper generator

Build backgrounds from scratch with gradients, meshes and patterns. No source image needed.

Curated collections

Nature, abstract and mesh-gradient images, ready to layer.

My library

Every edit, favourite, and recent wallpaper in one place.

Live preview

Drag any slider, see it update instantly. What you see is what applies.

Device-aware crops

Phones, foldables and tablets each get the right composition.
